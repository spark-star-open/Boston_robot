===== STREAM 17 =====
Introduction The basic pipeline extracts the point cloud for each object from the segmentation output as in the existing work, and then estimates the 6D pose by ICP - based matching. However, the problem is that the reliability of the segmentation stage determines the overall pipeline quality. Therefore, we propose a hybrid quantum - classical structure that reduces the false positive by trialing the mask around each patch using the candidate mask obtained with Classical Convolutional Neural Network and enhanced by Quantum Convolutional Neural Network. It is expected to be combined with classical hardware in the current generation of quantum hardware to improve segmentation classification quality. Abstract

===== STREAM 95 =====
Discussion 1. YOLO, U - Net CNNs Trying out existing classical models to experimentally observe image segmentation and classification Comparison of classical models leading image segmenting CNN to be flexible from modification Extension of classical CNN model to Quantum model 1. Hybrid QCNN - CNN model Built up a model that has both CNN and QCNN conducting segmentation and classification Quantum layer includes feature encoding, as well as parametrized circuit to introduce non - linear characteristics to classical model resulting in better performance on segmentation 2. Further Proposals Iterative circuit measurement requires huge amount of waiting time on IBM platform queue, this problem could be further handled (or might be mitigated by technology develpment) Since we have shown that applying quantum layer results in better accuracy, this method could be extended to complex segmentation jobs
